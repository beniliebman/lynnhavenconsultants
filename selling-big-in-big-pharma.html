The masterclass

Take Your Learning Further With the Selling Big in Big Pharma Masterclass.

Built on the book, the masterclass takes you through the entire range of selling skills needed for a successful career in pharmaceutical sales. It is the same sales training Jim used for thirty years with the people he brought into the industry.

  • 90 minutes of content
  • Short, easy-to-follow segments
  • Lifetime access

  1. Territory Organization

    Plan and organize your territory so your time goes where it counts.

  2. Outer-Office Procedure

    Work effectively with office staff before you ever see the physician.

  3. Approaches

    Open each call in a way that earns your customer’s attention.

  4. Presenting

    Deliver a clear, focused presentation of your product.

  5. Feature / Benefit

    Connect what your product does to why it matters to your customer.

  6. Offering Evidence

    Support your message with credible proof.

  7. Getting Feedback

    Check understanding and read your customer’s response as you go.

  8. Questioning

    Ask the questions that uncover what your customer needs.

  9. Listening

    Hear what your customer is really telling you.

  10. Overcoming Objections

    Respond to concerns with confidence.

  11. Trial Close / Close

    Recognize when to ask for the commitment, and ask for it.

  12. Exit and Follow-Up

    Leave every call well and set up the next one.
